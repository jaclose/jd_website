Preserved as the original square application icon; active views now use the vector orb below. This is brand artwork, not a product screenshot or a claim about released features.

Orb lighting and rim are a bespoke adaptation of the supplied circular reference, not a separate recovered raster asset. Used in active cards, the Sanctum greenhouse, and the journal. The former square icon remains archived.
